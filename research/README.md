# Senda Research Library

This directory is the working research library for Senda. It keeps market, corridor, customer, competitor, payment-provider and regulatory research alongside the product repository so research decisions remain connected to the product being built.

## Current focus

**Pilot corridor:** UK → Nigeria

Zimbabwe is not a current pilot market because Flutterwave does not currently support Zimbabwe transfers in the relevant product scope.

## Research framework

For each corridor, assess:

1. Market
2. UK diaspora / addressable customer population
3. Remittance evidence
4. Provider landscape
5. Pricing and FX
6. Payout methods
7. Flutterwave compatibility
8. Regulatory considerations
9. Senda-specific opportunity gap

## Evidence standard

Prefer primary and authoritative sources. Record the source, date, geography, population/sample where relevant, and limitations. Separate documented facts from estimates, analysis and assumptions.

## Primary research

Do not run a broad survey yet. First use secondary research to narrow the genuinely interesting customer/corridor combinations. Then create a short targeted survey to answer only questions public data cannot reliably answer, particularly multi-recipient behaviour, number of separate payments, recurring commitments, tracking pain and reaction to a grouped remittance order.
