# Senda Market Intelligence

**Status:** Secondary research phase
**Current pilot:** UK → Nigeria
**Last updated:** 17 September 2026

## Objective

Determine which customer/corridor combinations are worth putting in front of customers before investing heavily in primary research.

Senda's core proposition is a grouped remittance order: one customer payment funds an order containing multiple recipient commitments, while each recipient payout is independently tracked and reconciled.

## Research questions

- Is there a meaningful population of UK remitters supporting multiple people in the same destination country?
- How frequently do these customers remit and how many separate payments do they make?
- What are the main current providers and payout methods?
- What does the customer actually pay, including transfer fees and FX effects?
- Which corridors can be supported operationally and compliantly through Flutterwave or another suitable payment/payout structure?
- Where does Senda add value beyond price competition?

## Existing evidence to build on

### UK remittance market

World Bank data provides remittance-flow datasets and Remittance Prices Worldwide corridor data. Migration Observatory research provides UK-specific context, including remitter behaviour and historical bilateral estimates.

### Important strategic observation

Senda should not assume that competing primarily on price is the opportunity. Existing providers can be very competitive on individual transfers. The differentiated hypothesis to test is the management of multiple commitments: one customer payment, several recipient payouts, and a single place to track the resulting obligations and statuses.

## Evidence gaps

Public data is unlikely to reliably answer:

- typical number of regular recipients per UK remitter;
- number of separate payments made during a normal remittance cycle;
- proportion of remitters with recurring support commitments;
- how customers currently track multiple payments;
- whether a grouped order materially reduces friction;
- which specific customer segment values this most.

These should be answered through a short targeted survey and a small number of follow-up interviews only after the corridor screen is narrowed.

## Research discipline

Do not treat historical bilateral figures as current rankings. Do not use GitHub datasets as a substitute for authoritative current market evidence unless their provenance and date are clear. Use GitHub primarily for reproducible analysis, methodology and product-relevant technical research.
