# Senda Research Sources

This is the source register for the Senda market-intelligence work. Add a row whenever new evidence is incorporated into the research.

| Source | Topic | Geography | Date / period | Evidence used | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| World Bank — Remittance Prices Worldwide | Corridor pricing | Global / UK corridors | Dataset through Q1 2025 | Corridor cost, provider and pricing comparisons | Historical observation; individual provider quotes can differ |
| World Bank — Remittance Flows | Remittance flows | Global | 2000–2024 | Market scale and flows | Does not directly describe individual customer behaviour |
| Migration Observatory — Migrant remittances to and from the UK | UK remittance market | UK | Published 12 Mar 2025; behaviour data includes 2021/22 and historical bilateral estimates | UK remitter behaviour, costs and historical destination estimates | Bilateral estimates after 2021 are not available; some figures are estimates |
| ONS — Population estimates by grouped country of birth and nationality, UK 2024 | Diaspora | UK | 2024 | Current population context | Grouped categories may not map exactly to remittance customers |
| Flutterwave Developer Documentation | Payment / payout infrastructure | Supported markets | Current documentation | Collections, transfers, bank/mobile-money payout capabilities | Product support and commercial eligibility must be confirmed with Flutterwave |
| FCA payment services guidance | Regulation | UK | Current guidance | Receiving and transmitting customer money may constitute regulated payment services | Exact Senda structure requires legal/compliance assessment |

## Research rule

For each new source record the URL, publication date or data period, geography, population/sample where applicable, exact metric used, and any important limitation. Prefer primary sources and official datasets.
