# Senda 4.0 — Research agent briefs

**Human owns:** in-person / live interviews, intros, school visits, MTO meetings.  
**Agents own:** secondary research, ICPs, interview kits, synthesis into MVP product decisions, research log updates.

---

## Demand Research Agent

**Goal:** Max information on who buys Connect (MTOs, banks, remit brands) and what product shape they will underwrite — enough to freeze MVP.

**Do**
- Map ICP tiers (primary / stretch / adjacent)
- Tear down existing purpose/bill/business-pay products (Remitly Business Pay, Mukuru Bill Payment, PayAngel D2MR, Wise, WorldRemit)
- List buyer objections (risk, custody, SLA, density thresholds)
- Draft interview guide + screener for founder calls
- Feed **must-have / nice / out** into MVP PRD

**Don’t**
- Pretend primary interviews happened
- Pitch adaptor-as-payer as the product
- Expand into full remittance competitor analysis beyond purpose-settlement relevance

**Outputs**
- `docs/customer-research/demand-mto-pack.md`
- Updates to `docs/product/mvp-prd.md` (demand implications section)

---

## Supply Research Agent

**Goal:** Max information on schools/billers (NG first; ZW notes) — how fees are paid today, KYB friction, settle destinations, proof needs — enough to design Biller MVP.

**Do**
- Map school segments and current stacks (bank + ref, Remita, EduPay/VA, portals)
- Document diaspora pain / scam patterns from public sources
- ZW rails note (EcoCash, InnBucks, SmileLink) without making ZW the pilot by default
- Draft bursar/admin interview guide
- Feed onboarding + Link + proof requirements into MVP PRD

**Don’t**
- Require SIS integrations for MVP
- Assume Flutterwave/EcoCash adaptor-as-payer

**Outputs**
- `docs/customer-research/supply-school-pack.md`
- Updates to `docs/product/mvp-prd.md` (supply implications section)
